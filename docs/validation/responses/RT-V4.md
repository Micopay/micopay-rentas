## 2. After the full README — what did you get wrong?

- What did you misunderstand in step 1, or what surprised you?
  > At first, I thought the host would receive my money immediately after I booked. I was surprised to learn that the money stays in escrow until I check in. I think this is a good way to protect both sides.
- What is still unclear?
  > I also learned that getting a refund depends on the cancellation policy, so I would want to understand those rules before paying.

## 4. Value

- **How useful does it look to you?** 1 (not at all) – 5 (very). Why?
  > I would rate it 4 out of 5.
- What is the most valuable part?
  > The most useful part for me is the escrow system because it gives guests more confidence that their money is protected until check-in.
- What is missing?
  > What's missing is more verified property photos, clear information about all fees, and an easy way to contact the host.
- What would stop you (or the person in your issue's role) from using it?
  > What would stop me from booking is unclear cancellation and refund rules, hidden charges, or not being sure that the property is genuine.

## 5. The MicoPay app

*The README section "How it connects to the MicoPay app (planned)" describes the guest paying from the MicoPay app and the host cashing out with an agent or via SPEI.*

- Does that connection make the product more useful to you? What would it change for you?
  > I would say yes, it would make the app more useful. Being able to pay, approve a booking, and request or approve a deposit refund all inside MicoPay would make the process easier and more convenient. It would also help me keep track of my payments and bookings in one place. I would feel more confident using it as long as the app is secure and explains every transaction clearly.

## 6. What confused you

- Which screen, step or term confused you, and what did you expect instead?
  > What confused me most was the deposit refund process. I wasn't completely sure what conditions must be met before the deposit is returned, how long the refund takes, or what happens if the host and guest disagree about damage or other issues. I think the app should explain these rules clearly and show the steps for resolving disputes so guests know what to expect.

## 7. Questions for your issue

- Walk through finding a stay, seeing the price breakdown and booking (in the demo or from the README): what is confusing or missing?
  > What confuses me most is knowing exactly how much I will pay before confirming the booking. I would like to see a clear breakdown of the stay price, cleaning fee, 3% service fee, and deposit.
- Is it clear what you pay for (stay, cleaning, fee, deposit) and what comes back?
  > I also want to understand how the peso price is converted into USDC, whether the exchange rate can change, and when I will receive my deposit back after checkout.
- What would you expect to see after booking?
  > Right after booking, I would expect a confirmation message showing that my reservation was successful, the property details, check-in and check-out dates, and the full amount paid. I would also like to see a breakdown of the stay cost, cleaning fee, 3% fee, and deposit, including how much was paid in USDC. It would be helpful to receive the host's contact information, check-in instructions, and clear information about when and how my deposit will be refunded.

## 8. Ideas

Up to 3. For each one: the problem it solves, then the idea.

1. **Problem:** Guests don't know what has been completed and what they need to do next. · **Idea:** Booking progress tracker: Show each stage of the booking, from payment to host confirmation and check-in.
2. **Problem:** Confusion and misunderstandings about cancellations and refunds. · **Idea:** Clear cancellation and refund timeline: Explain the cancellation rules and show when a guest can expect their money or deposit back.
3. **Problem:** Delays at check-in. · **Idea:** Check-in checklist: Provide simple instructions and reminders about check-in time, location, access codes, and what to do when arriving, to make check-in smoother.
