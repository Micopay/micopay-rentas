## 1. First read — what do you think it is?

*Read the README only up to its first `##` heading. Write this before reading anything else.*

- What does MicoPay Rentas do, in your own words?

- Who is it for?
  > People who want to rent houses for a short period, and property owners who want to rent out their homes.

  > It helps make payments safer by keeping the guest's booking money in escrow until check-in. It also helps protect both guests and hosts by providing clear cancellation rules and damage deposit terms. It makes renting easier and more trustworthy for everyone.

## 3. Short-term rentals where you live

- Your country / general region (no city or address needed).
  > Nigeria (North Central).
- Where you live, how do people rent homes for short stays? (Airbnb, Booking, other platforms, social media groups, renting directly…)
  > In Nigeria, people usually rent a place for a few days through agents, social media, online platforms or directly from the property owner. Most people pay through bank transfers or sometimes cash.
- What fees do guests or hosts notice, and what do they think of them? A percentage is fine; no amounts of money.
  > People often complain about high agent fees, service charges, cleaning fees, hidden costs and unexpected deposits.
- Do people book directly with the host? What makes them trust or distrust doing that?
  > Some people trust paying the owner directly because they can communicate with the owner and avoid extra agent fees. However, others don't trust it because of scammers, fake property listings and the fear of losing money without getting a refund.
- Are there common problems with cancellations, damage deposits or disputes?
  > Some people lose money because of fake agents, scammers or misleading pictures of properties. Sometimes people pay before seeing the apartment and later discover that it is not real. This makes people worry about trusting strangers when booking a place.
- Do you live this yourself (as a guest or host), see it up close, or only hear about it?
  > I have mostly heard about short-stay rentals and seen other people's experiences.

## 7. Questions for your issue

- Using only the README, answer: if the guest cancels before check-in, who decides how much comes back, and does the damage deposit come back?
  > If the guest cancels, the refund depends on the cancellation policy, but the damage deposit is always returned.
- If the host cancels, what does the guest get back?
  > If the host cancels, the guest gets all their money back.
- If nobody does anything after the stay, what happens to the money?
  > If nobody acts after the stay, the host receives the payment and the guest gets the damage deposit back.
- Which of these was hard to work out, and why?
  > The part that confused me most was how MicoPay handles disputes and decides how the money is shared between the guest, host and platform.
